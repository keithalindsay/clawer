## Header Image Concept

**Visual direction:** Dark background (#0a0a0a or deep navy), lobster emoji large and centered or offset-left, text on the right.

**Layout:**
- Left half: Large 🦞 emoji (display size ~200px)
- Right half:
  - Line 1: `clawer.ai` in clean white sans-serif (large)
  - Line 2: `Managed AI Teams` in lighter gray
  - Line 3: Small icons for WhatsApp, Telegram, or a team silhouette graphic

**Color palette:**
- Background: `#0d0d0d` (near-black)
- Primary text: `#ffffff`
- Accent: `#ff4500` (ProductHunt orange) or a warm coral/lobster red — `#e8523a`
- Subtext: `#888888`

**Mood:** Clean, builder aesthetic. Not startup-bubbly. Not enterprise-cold. Somewhere between "Vercel" and "Indie Hacker."

**Size:** 1500 × 500px (Twitter header spec)

**Tools to make it:** Figma, Canva (use their "Twitter Header" template), or just drop into Ray.so if you want a code-screenshot aesthetic.

**Alternative concept:** Screenshot of the actual AI team dashboard with a subtle overlay and the logo in the corner. Real product > abstract design on launch day.
