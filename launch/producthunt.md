## Full Description

### The problem

Every AI tool wants you to chat with a single agent. You write the prompt. You wait. You copy the output. You start over.

That works for a question. It doesn't work for a business.

Running a business means research AND writing AND scheduling AND customer replies AND a hundred other things happening at the same time. A single AI assistant is like hiring one employee and asking them to do everything — sequentially, forever.

### The solution: clawer.ai

**clawer.ai** is a managed AI Teams platform. Instead of one agent, you get a coordinated crew — each with a specific role, working together to get things done.

Built on OpenClaw, deployed for you. You don't touch a server. You don't write a Dockerfile. You log in, pick your team, connect your phone, and go.

### What makes it different

**🤖 AI Teams, not single agents**
8 pre-built team templates for real use cases: Content Studio, Research Team, Customer Support Crew, E-Commerce Manager, and more. Each team has specialized roles — a researcher who hands off to a writer who hands off to a publisher.

**📱 WhatsApp & Telegram built in**
Text your AI team the same way you text your friends. Ask questions, assign tasks, get updates — from your phone, wherever you are.

**🏠 Managed hosting — zero setup**
Your team runs on our infrastructure, isolated in its own container. No AWS account. No Render free tier timeouts. No "why did it crash at 3am." Just works.

**🔒 Security-first**
Each workspace is container-isolated. Your data doesn't share space with anyone else's. We take multi-tenancy seriously.

**📋 Task board (coming this week)**
Assign tasks to your team, track progress, see what's done. Like a Trello board, but the people on it are AIs who actually finish things.
