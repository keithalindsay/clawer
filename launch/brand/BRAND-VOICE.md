# Clawer.ai Brand Voice Guide
*Last updated: 2026-02-23*

---

## Tone Attributes

### 1. Straight-Talking
**Definition:** Say it plainly. No jargon walls, no corporate fluff, no "leveraging synergies." If something is hard, say it's hard. If something sucks, say it sucks.
- ✅ "Self-hosting OpenClaw is a pain. We fixed that."
- ❌ "We've streamlined the deployment experience to optimize operational efficiency."

### 2. Engineer-Honest
**Definition:** We're builders, not marketers. We acknowledge tradeoffs. We show our work. We don't pretend everything is perfect — we explain why it's *better*.
- ✅ "OpenClaw is incredible software. Running it yourself? That's the hard part."
- ❌ "Clawer.ai is the revolutionary next-generation AI platform transforming businesses worldwide."

### 3. Casually Confident
**Definition:** We know what we built is good. We don't need to scream about it. The tone is "friend who's really good at this stuff explaining it over coffee."
- ✅ "Your agents are live in 60 seconds. That's it. Go do something else."
- ❌ "Our industry-leading deployment pipeline achieves sub-minute provisioning times!!"

### 4. Security-Serious
**Definition:** When it comes to security, we drop the casualness. We cite specifics — CVEs, numbers, real incidents. This is where we earn trust.
- ✅ "42,000+ OpenClaw instances are exposed on port 18789 with no auth. Ours aren't."
- ❌ "We take security very seriously." (Never say this. Everyone says this. It means nothing.)

### 5. Anti-Hype
**Definition:** The AI space is drowning in hype. We're the antidote. No "AGI," no "paradigm shift," no "transformative." Just: here's what it does, here's who it's for, here's what it costs.
- ✅ "AI agents that handle your tasks while you sleep. $49/month."
- ❌ "Unlock the transformative potential of autonomous AI-powered digital workforce solutions."

## Voice Do's and Don'ts

### DO: Lead with the pain point
- **Before:** "Clawer.ai offers managed OpenClaw hosting with enterprise-grade security features."
- **After:** "Tired of babysitting your OpenClaw server at 2am? We run it for you."

### DO: Use specific numbers
- **Before:** "Deploy quickly with minimal setup."
- **After:** "Deploy in 60 seconds. 100 free messages. No credit card."

### DO: Acknowledge the DIY crowd respectfully
- **Before:** "Stop wasting time with self-hosting."
- **After:** "Self-hosting is great if you love it. If you'd rather skip the Docker debugging, we're here."

### DON'T: Use corporate passive voice
- **Before:** "Your deployment will be provisioned automatically."
- **After:** "Hit deploy. Your agents go live."

### DON'T: Oversell or overpromise
- **Before:** "Clawer.ai will revolutionize your entire business!"
- **After:** "Clawer.ai runs your AI agents so you don't have to manage servers."

### DON'T: Talk down to non-technical users
- **Before:** "Simply configure your Docker compose file and set environment variables."
- **After:** "Pick a team, connect your chat app, hit deploy."

### DON'T: Use fear-mongering without facts
- **Before:** "Your data is at RISK if you self-host!!!"
- **After:** "CVE-2026-25253 exposed 42K instances. Here's what we do differently."

## Vocabulary Guide

### Words We Use → Words We Avoid
| We Say | We Don't Say |
|--------|-------------|
| AI team | AI workforce / digital employees |
| agents | bots (unless quoting others) |
| deploy | provision / instantiate |
| skills | plugins / extensions / modules |
| channels | touchpoints / endpoints |
| managed hosting | cloud infrastructure solution |
| setup | onboarding journey |
| works with | integrates seamlessly with |
| security-first | enterprise-grade security |
| container isolation | sandboxed execution environment |
| plain English | natural language interface |
| breaks / broken | suboptimal experience |
| costs | investment |
| $49/month | competitive pricing |

### Banned Phrases (Never Use)
- "We take security very seriously"
- "Leveraging AI to..."
- "Best-in-class"
- "Cutting-edge"
- "Paradigm shift"
- "Digital transformation"
- "Unlock the power of..."
- "Seamless" (overused to meaninglessness)
- "Delighted to announce"
- "Game-changer"
- "Disruptive"
- "Synergy" (in any form)

### Encouraged Phrases
- "While you sleep" (our signature)
- "Not a chatbot. An AI team."
- "60 seconds"
- "No Docker. No servers. No config files."
- "Wake up to done"
- "The hard part, handled"

## Platform-Specific Tone

### Keith's Founder Voice (@Vavier on X)
**Tone:** Self-deprecating engineer humor, honest about the grind, opinionated about tech, relatable founder moments.
**Rules:**
- First person always ("I built...", "I spent 6 hours...")
- Personal observations and hot takes welcome
- Self-deprecation is a feature, not a bug
- Can reference Clawer but shouldn't be a constant pitch
- Engage genuinely with the OpenClaw community
- Short, punchy. One idea per tweet.

**Sample Tweets:**
1. "Spent 6 hours optimizing a query that saves users 0.3 seconds. Spent 0 hours on marketing. This is why engineers shouldn't run companies. I am the engineer."
2. "Hot take: 90% of AI wrappers will die. The survivors will be the ones who picked one thing and made it stupidly easy. That's the whole strategy."
3. "The bottleneck in AI products isn't the model. It's the 47 decisions users have to make before they get value. Cut 46 of them."
4. "Debugging at 2am hits different. Either you're a genius or you're about to rm -rf production. No in-between."
5. "Founder life: someone emails 'love the product!' and you ride that high for 3 days. Someone emails 'it crashed' and you don't sleep for 2."

### Brand Voice (@teamclawer on X)
**Tone:** Helpful, direct, informative. Less personal humor, more "here's something useful." Still human — not a corporate account.
**Rules:**
- Third person or imperative ("Deploy your...", "Your AI team...")
- Lead with value: tips, guides, security alerts, use cases
- Respond to community questions genuinely
- Share blog posts with a hook, not just a link
- Retweet/quote Keith sparingly but authentically

**Sample Tweets:**
1. "42,000 OpenClaw instances are sitting on the open internet with no auth. Don't be one of them. [link to security post]"
2. "New on the blog: The honest cost comparison of self-hosting vs managed OpenClaw. We did the math. You might be surprised. [link]"
3. "Shipped: WhatsApp channel support. Connect with a QR code. Your agents reply in <2 seconds. No Twilio, no webhook config."
4. "Your AGENTS.md is the most important file in your OpenClaw setup and most people never edit it. Here's what you're missing → [thread]"
5. "Not a chatbot. An AI team. One agent researches. Another drafts. A third schedules. They work together while you sleep."

### Blog Voice
**Tone:** Authoritative but accessible. The knowledgeable friend who writes really clear documentation. Can go deep technically but always explains *why* something matters.
**Rules:**
- Use "you" freely — talk directly to the reader
- Open with the problem or a hook, never with "In this article we will..."
- Include specific numbers, CVEs, real examples
- Code blocks where relevant, but explain what they do
- End with a clear CTA (try Clawer, read the next post, check your config)
- Subheadings every 2-3 paragraphs for scannability
- Acknowledge that we're a hosting provider — be transparent about the bias, then prove the point with data anyway
