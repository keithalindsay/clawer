1. Post Option A as your main reply — it's the safest and most authentic
2. Engage with any genuine responses to your reply
3. If someone asks "what's clawer.ai?" — that's your opening: give a 2-sentence answer and link
4. Don't post all three options — pick one and be a person, not a marketing machine
5. Time it: post within the first 2-3 hours of VidClaw's launch when traffic is highest
