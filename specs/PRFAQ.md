### What does "Bring Your Own Keys" (BYOK) mean?

You can plug in your own API keys from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, whoever. When you do:

1. **You pay providers directly** — No markup, no Clawer taking a cut on API calls
2. **Your data goes straight to the source** — Clawer never sees your message content, just routes it
3. **You control the relationship** — Using an enterprise OpenAI contract? Academic credits? Promotional credits? Bring them.
4. **No lock-in** — You're not trapped. You can leave anytime and keep your API relationship.

If you don't want to manage API keys, Clawer provides pooled access (with usage limits on Free tier). Most people start pooled, switch to BYOK when they're sending 500+ messages/month and want to optimize costs.

Either way, you win. This is the opposite of vendor lock-in.

### Who owns my data? Is it private?

**You own it.** Not "technically you own it but we have a license to do whatever." You. Own. It.

Free tier: Shared infrastructure (keeps costs low), but your data is encrypted and isolated. We can't read it, and other users can't touch it.

Pro tier: Isolated container. Your assistant runs in its own environment. No shared databases, no nosy neighbors, no cross-contamination.

BYOK users: Your messages go straight from Clawer's router to OpenAI/Anthropic/whoever. We never see the content. Just metadata (timestamps, message counts for billing).

**We don't train models on your conversations.** Ever. That's in the Terms of Service, not buried in a footnote.

Want to leave? Export your full conversation history, download your data, migrate to self-hosted OpenClaw. We'll even give you a migration guide. No hostages.

If you've ever worried about an AI service reading your private conversations or selling your data, this is built for you.

### How does the Smart Router work?

Every message you send gets analyzed in ~50 milliseconds:

**Simple/fast tasks** ("remind me at 3pm," "what's the weather") → GPT-4o-mini ($0.0001 per message)  
**Complex work** ("write a strategy doc," "debug this code") → Claude Opus or GPT-4 ($0.01-0.03 per message)  
**Creative tasks** ("brainstorm video ideas") → Models optimized for creativity

You never think about it. The router just picks the cheapest model that won't screw up your task.

If you were doing this manually, you'd either:
- Use GPT-4 for everything and burn $200/month, or
- Use cheap models for everything and get garbage outputs

The Smart Router saves you ~$150/month compared to "always use the best model" and doesn't make you babysit every request. It's the difference between a $49 bill and a $250 surprise.

### Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. One click. No "are you sure?" guilt trips. No cancellation fees. No calling a phone number.

You keep access until the end of your billing period. Export your data if you want. If you decide to self-host OpenClaw, we'll send you a migration guide (and a paid migration service if you want help, but the guide is free).

We don't win by trapping you. We win by being so much easier than the alternative that you'd rather pay $49 than deal with servers.

### Why not just self-host OpenClaw?

You should! If you're technical, have time, and want absolute control, OpenClaw is open source and free.

But self-hosting means:

- **Finding a VPS** (DigitalOcean? Hetzner? Linode? Which one? What specs?)
- **Configuring OpenClaw** (Environment variables. SSH keys. Docker compose files that break for no reason.)
- **Managing API keys** (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google—12 different dashboards)
- **Setting up messaging integrations** (WhatsApp Business API is a nightmare. Telegram is easier but still requires a bot token and webhook config.)
- **Updates** (New OpenClaw version drops. Do you trust it? Do you test it first? Do you have rollback ready?)
- **Debugging** (It stops working. You spend 2 hours figuring out Redis ran out of memory.)

If your time is worth $50/hour, you'll spend $200+ in setup and $20/month in ongoing maintenance. Or pay Clawer $49/month and never think about it.

Choose based on what you value: control or time. Both are legitimate. We're here for the people who pick time.

### What are the biggest risks and how do we survive them?

**1. OpenClaw dependency**

Risk: Development slows, breaking changes, license change.  
Mitigation: We contribute to OpenClaw (influence roadmap), can fork if needed, hiring core contributors if we scale. Worst case: we maintain a fork. Not ideal, but survivable.

**2. AI provider costs spike**

Risk: OpenAI/Anthropic raise API prices 3x, margins collapse.  
Mitigation: BYOK passes costs to users. Smart Router optimizes pooled usage. Multi-provider support (can shift to Google, open-source models). If prices spike, BYOK adoption goes up, margins stay healthy.

**3. Users don't trust us (SimpleClaw redux)**

Risk: "Another hosted service that's gonna rug pull or sell my data."  
Mitigation: Open source foundation, BYOK, isolated containers, export/migration path. If we can't earn trust, we die. This is the bet. Trust = moat.

**4. Messaging platforms kill us**

Risk: WhatsApp bans AI bots, Telegram restricts usage, policies change.  
Mitigation: Multi-platform (lose WhatsApp, still have Telegram/Slack/Web). Personal account integration, not just Business API. Responsive to policy changes. Diversification protects us.

**5. ChatGPT adds teams/memory/integrations**

Risk: They copy our model, bundle it for $25/month, we're dead.  
Mitigation: We'll have 12-month head start, OpenClaw community loyalty, BYOK (ChatGPT will never do this), and template marketplace network effects. If they come for us, we go deeper on open source and customization. Race to the bottom on price, we lose. Race to the top on flexibility, we win.

**6. Free tier abuse**

Risk: Users spam accounts to get unlimited free messages.  
Mitigation: Phone/email verification, device fingerprinting, rate limiting, ML abuse detection. Cost of acquisition > cost of some abuse. Don't over-optimize for edge cases.
