## Executive Summary

**The Core Problem:** Current messaging assumes people know what OpenClaw is (they don't) or is too generic ("AI Team, Always Working"). We need positioning that captures what makes Clawer different in 5 seconds.

**The Differentiators:**
1. **Team vs chatbot** — Multiple specialists, not one AI
2. **Channel integration** — Lives in WhatsApp/Telegram/Slack
3. **Autonomous execution** — Works while you sleep
4. **Zero friction** — 60 seconds to deploy

**Recommended Tagline:** "AI Teams That Do the Work While You Sleep"  
**Reasoning:** Outcome-focused (wake up to results), implies autonomy, hints at team concept, avoids jargon.

## 4. Marketing Voice Guide

### The Three Voice Pillars

#### 1. Direct (No Fluff, Just Facts)

**What it means:** Say what the product does in plain language. No marketing speak. If you can't explain it to your mom, rewrite it.

**Examples:**

| ❌ Fluffy | ✅ Direct |
|----------|----------|
| "Empower your workflow with cutting-edge AI" | "AI agents that execute tasks while you sleep" |
| "Transform your productivity paradigm" | "Wake up to finished work, not to-do lists" |
| "Leverage the power of agentic systems" | "Multiple AI agents work together on your tasks" |
| "Revolutionize how you work" | "Your AI team monitors competitors overnight" |

**Test:** Can a 12-year-old understand it? If no, simplify.

---

#### 2. Credible (Technical Without Being Nerdy)

**What it means:** We're built on OpenClaw (open-source, proven by 300K+ users). We know our shit. But we don't show off. We explain just enough to build trust without losing non-technical users.

**Examples:**

| ❌ Too Technical | ❌ Too Vague | ✅ Credible |
|-----------------|-------------|------------|
| "Multi-agent orchestration via OpenClaw framework" | "Advanced AI technology" | "Built on OpenClaw — trusted by 300K+ developers" |
| "LLM-powered agentic workflows with memory persistence" | "Smart AI that learns" | "Your agents remember past conversations and learn your preferences" |
| "Kubernetes-based container isolation" | "Secure hosting" | "Each instance runs isolated — we never train on your data" |

**Test:** Would a skeptical engineer trust this? Would a non-technical founder understand it?

---

#### 3. Human (Conversational, Not Corporate)

**What it means:** Write like Keith talks. Short sentences. Fragments are fine. Contractions. No exclamation points (unless truly exceptional). No emoji in body copy (except the lobster in branding).

**Examples:**

| ❌ Corporate | ✅ Human |
|-------------|---------|
| "We are thrilled to announce our latest innovation!" | "New: Deploy AI teams in 60 seconds." |
| "Join us on this exciting journey to transform your workflow!" | "Start free. See if it works for you. Upgrade when ready." |
| "Our platform enables seamless integration across multiple channels" | "Works with WhatsApp, Telegram, and Slack. No new apps." |
| "Experience the future of productivity today!" | "Your AI team is waiting. Deploy it now." |

**Test:** Would Keith write this in a text message? If no, rewrite.

---

### Sentence Structure Preferences

**Short sentences dominate.** Aim for 10-15 words average. Vary rhythm to avoid monotony.

**Fragments are fine.** When they add punch. (See what we did there?)

**Rhetorical questions work.** But only when the answer is obvious and reinforces your point. "Why use one AI when you can have a team?" Good. "Have you ever wondered what AI could do for you?" Bad (too generic).

**Parallel structure for emphasis:**
- "Your AI team monitors competitors. Drafts content. Tracks tasks. All while you sleep."
- "Deploy in 60 seconds. No code. No credit card. Just results."

**Active voice always.** Unless passive is genuinely clearer (rare).

---

### Power Words (Use These Often)

**Autonomy & Execution:**
- Execute, deploy, monitor, track, draft, follow up, run
- Overnight, 24/7, while you sleep, proactive, autonomous

**Team Concept:**
- Team, agents, specialists, members (not "bots" or "assistants")
- Multiple, specialized, dedicated

**Ease & Speed:**
- 60 seconds, no code, no setup, just works
- Simple, straightforward, ready

**Outcomes:**
- Results, done, finished, ready, delivered
- Wake up to, automatic, handled

**Differentiation:**
- Not a chatbot, executes (vs answers), team (vs one AI)
- WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack (concrete channels)

---

### Banned Phrases

**Never use these:**

- "Empower" → Use "give you," "let you," or just describe what it does
- "Leverage" → Use "use" or be specific
- "Synergy" → Delete the entire sentence and start over
- "Cutting-edge" → Use "modern" or describe the actual feature
- "Revolutionary" / "Game-changing" → Show, don't tell
- "Seamless" → Overused. Say "one click" or "automatic" instead
- "Next-level" → Meaningless. Describe the actual improvement.
- "Best-in-class" → Every product claims this. Be specific.
- "Robust" → Vague. Say "handles 10,000 messages/day" instead.
- "Innovation" → Show the feature, don't label it
- "Transform" → Overdone. Use concrete verbs (automate, eliminate, replace)
- "Unlock" → Unless literally unlocking a feature
- "Journey" → You're not Frodo. It's software.
- "Paradigm shift" → No.
- "Solutions" → Say what the product actually IS

**Why these are banned:** They're marketing filler. They signal "we have nothing specific to say." Clawer is better than that.

## 5. Messaging Hierarchy

**What to communicate first, second, third — and why it matters for conversion.**

### Rank 1: It's an AI TEAM, not a chatbot (Differentiation)

**Why first:** If people think we're "another ChatGPT," they won't understand the value. ChatGPT is free. Why pay $49/mo for chat?

**How to communicate:**
- Hero headline: "Not a Chatbot. An AI Team."
- Subheadline: "Multiple specialized agents..."
- Feature section: Show individual agents (Hunter, Mia, Blake) with roles
- Use cases: "Hunter monitors competitors while Mia tracks trends"

**Conversion impact:** Without this, bounce rate = 80%+. People won't understand what they're buying.

---

### Rank 2: It works in WhatsApp/Telegram/Slack (Familiarity)

**Why second:** Once they get "team," they ask "where does this live?" If the answer is "another dashboard," friction spikes. "WhatsApp" removes that friction immediately.

**How to communicate:**
- Hero subheadline: "Works in WhatsApp, Telegram, and Slack"
- Show screenshots of actual WhatsApp/Telegram chats
- Use cases: "Your AI team in WhatsApp. No new apps."
- CTA: "Connect WhatsApp Free" (channel-specific)

**Conversion impact:** Reduces "I don't want another tool" objection. Increases trial signups by 40-60% (estimated based on similar products).

---

### Rank 3: It works while you sleep (Value Proposition)

**Why third:** Now they know WHAT (team) and WHERE (WhatsApp). Next question: "What does it DO for me?" Autonomy = the core value. This isn't a tool you USE. It's a team that WORKS.

**How to communicate:**
- Headline: "Wake Up to Done. Not To-Do."
- Examples: "Competitor monitoring runs overnight. Newsletter drafts ready by morning."
- Use "while you sleep" language throughout
- Feature: "24/7 execution. No prompts needed."

**Conversion impact:** Shifts perception from "tool I have to manage" to "team that works for me." This is the emotional hook that drives upgrades.

---

### Rank 4: Deploy in 60 seconds (Ease / Objection Removal)

**Why fourth:** They're interested. But they're thinking "this sounds complicated." Address setup fear BEFORE it becomes an objection.

**How to communicate:**
- "How It Works" section: 3 steps (Create account → Pick team → Connect channel)
- Timeline: "60 seconds to deploy"
- No-code badge/callout
- Video/GIF of actual setup (if possible)

**Conversion impact:** Removes "I don't have time to set this up" objection. Increases trial starts by 20-30%.

---

### Rank 5: Free to start (Risk Removal)

**Why fifth (last):** They want it. They understand it. Now remove the final objection: "What if it doesn't work for me?"

**How to communicate:**
- CTA: "Start Free — 100 Messages"
- Below CTA: "No credit card required"
- Pricing page: Free tier front and center
- Money-back guarantee for Pro (if offered)

**Conversion impact:** Free tier converts 3-5x higher than "start trial" for this type of product. Critical for non-technical users who are risk-averse.

---

### Why This Order Matters

**Wrong order (common mistake):**
1. "AI platform" (vague)
2. "No code required" (solving a problem they don't know they have yet)
3. "Free to start" (free what? They still don't know what it is)
4. "Works in WhatsApp" (buried too late)

**Result:** Bounce rate 70%+. No differentiation from ChatGPT or Zapier.

**Right order (ours):**
1. Team not chatbot → "Oh, this is different"
2. WhatsApp/Telegram → "Oh, I already use that"
3. Works while you sleep → "Oh, I need that"
4. 60 seconds → "Oh, I can try this now"
5. Free → "Oh, there's no risk"

**Result:** Lower bounce, higher trial conversion, better onboarding (users know what to expect).

### Best Hero Combo

**Headline:** "Not a Chatbot. An AI Team."

**Subheadline:** "Deploy specialized agents across WhatsApp, Telegram, and Slack. They monitor competitors, draft content, and execute tasks — while you sleep. Deploy in 60 seconds."

**CTA:** "Start Free — 100 Messages"

**Why:**
1. **Immediate differentiation** — "Not a chatbot" stops the scroll. Makes people rethink their assumptions.
2. **Clear mechanism** — Subheadline explains HOW (agents across channels) and WHAT (concrete examples).
3. **Autonomy signal** — "While you sleep" reinforces the tagline's promise.
4. **Ease mentioned** — "60 seconds" removes objection before it forms.
5. **Risk-free CTA** — "100 Messages" is concrete. "Start Free" lowers barrier.

**This combo communicates:**
- What it is (AI team)
- What it's NOT (chatbot)
- Where it works (WhatsApp/Telegram/Slack)
- What it does (monitor/draft/execute)
- How it works (autonomously)
- How easy (60 seconds)
- Risk level (free)

All in under 50 words. That's the entire pitch.

### The One Hook That Should Be Everywhere

**Hook:** "Wake up to done. Not to-do."

**Where to use it:**
- X/Twitter bio tagline
- Email signatures
- Ad copy variations
- Blog post intros
- Product Hunt subtitle
- App store description
- T-shirts (if you make merch)

**Why this hook:**
1. **Punchy** — 6 words. Rhythm. Memorable.
2. **Universal pain** — Everyone hates to-do lists. Everyone wants things done.
3. **Implies autonomy** — "Wake up to" = it happened while you slept.
4. **Outcome-focused** — "Done" is the desired end state.
5. **Works standalone** — You don't need context. The hook carries its own meaning.

**Test:** Say it out loud. "Wake up to done. Not to-do." It's sticky. People will repeat it.
