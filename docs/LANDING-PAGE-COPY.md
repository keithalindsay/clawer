## FAQ Section

### Is this just a wrapper around ChatGPT?

No. Clawer is built on OpenClaw, which can use multiple AI models (GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, local LLMs). More importantly, it's not a chat interface — it's an AI that takes action. It connects to your tools, remembers context, and executes tasks.

### How is this different from DIY OpenClaw?

Same engine, zero ops. We handle hosting, updates, security patches, and scaling. You handle talking to it. Think Gmail vs. running your own mail server.

### Can I run this on my own infrastructure?

Yes. That's kind of the point for compliance-sensitive industries. You get full control, we provide the software and support.

### What about data privacy?

Your data is yours. We don't train on it. We don't sell it. Self-hosted options mean your data never leaves your network.

### What channels does it work on?

WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord — with more coming. One AI, accessible from wherever you already work.
